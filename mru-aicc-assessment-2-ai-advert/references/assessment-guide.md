# Assessment 2: AI advert review guide

Official source: framework page 1. Week 6, final AI advertisement for any chosen brand, /100. Read `rubric.json` for exact criteria and maxima. These prompts help interpret evidence; they add no separate sub-marks or required deliverables.

| Official criterion | Evidence to inspect | Useful feedback when weak |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creative concept and originality /20 | Central idea, execution choices, distinctiveness and how consistently the concept is developed. | Identify what feels generic and suggest one concrete creative choice that strengthens the concept. Do not declare copying without verified evidence. |
| Brand/product communication /20 | Can a viewer identify the brand/product and understand its key appeal/message? Inspect labels, text, spoken claims and the ending. | State which message or identification cue is unclear and where it should be made clearer. A CTA is not mandatory. |
| Storytelling and structure /15 | Progression, beginning/development/ending, pacing and the relation between shots and message. | Identify a specific confusing sequence, missing connection or pacing problem and how to restructure it. A non-narrative advert can still have coherent structure. |
| Visual quality and consistency /15 | Intentional composition, continuity, identity/product stability, legible text and distracting generation errors. | Cite actual frames/time ranges and the effect on the viewer. Avoid deducting for intentional stylization. |
| Use and integration of AI /10 | How AI elements contribute to the idea and fit into the final advert. Use supplied process evidence if available. | Explain which elements feel disconnected or underdeveloped. Do not infer exact tools, prompts, or provenance from appearance, and do not require extra process files absent from the brief. When provenance is uncertain, describe that uncertainty while scoring the observable integration. |
| Audio, voice and music /10 | Audible speech, suitable sound/music, mix balance, clipping, noise and continuity across edits. | Cite the audible issue and suggest a practical mix or timing change. Music or voice is not individually mandatory when the chosen sound design supports the advert. |
| Editing and final delivery /10 | Deliberate timing/cuts/titles/ending; completeness, playback and watchability. | Identify unfinished elements and the precise edit/export fix. Do not invent format or duration constraints. |

Assess the chosen brand fairly, including a student-created brand if identifiable. Avoid a preference for expensive tools or photorealism. Record any supplied campaign brief as additional context, and score its requirements only where the official rubric or instructor explicitly supports them.
